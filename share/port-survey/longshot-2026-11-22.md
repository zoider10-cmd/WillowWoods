**Summary.** The best of today's news for this PC is in the texture path and in a fix for something we took
yesterday. (1) 577bba12 fixes a freeze that #1458's held image (which we took 2026-11-21 as dd29e83a) causes on an RX
9070 XT under Windows: the picture stays frozen after Alt+Tab with frame generation on, until F11. (2) b9bd74a2 stops
"storms" of texture re-uploads (6-11 GB after closing the pause menu, fps 61-68 -> 75 there) caused by binding all 64
elements of the game's light table. Our tree has the same 64-element tables and the same "GPU wrote its storage:
uploaded again" path. (3) 658e5f2b rewrites the GPU ASTC decoder our card uses (AMD desktop GPUs have no ASTC
hardware): 5.3x faster decode, area-entry stalls down 4x, traversal 1% low +48.5% on a Radeon 860M. (4) aafc8381 finds
why our ASTC-to-BC runs must be one layer at a time on AMD's Windows driver (a multi-layer copy into a mip tail not a
multiple of 4 loses its edge blocks: the Hyrule Field grid lines our own comment describes). With it, 8-layer runs
come back. All four are renderer-only, touch no randomizer file, and are small or shader-only. Lower down: a 4-line
translator fix for flashing rocks (0e32bc10), the backflip fix above a 60 cap (the owner plays at 120), and a no-code
readback A/B measured on a 9070 XT under Linux.

## Ranked candidates

### 1. A held window image that never comes back makes the swapchain again (577bba12, #2083) - PORT FIRST (fixes our dd29e83a)
- What: #1458's hold (an image not free in 250 ms is held and nothing else is presented) can last forever on AMD's
  Windows driver. The system lets an image go only when another one is shown, and while one is held nothing else is
  shown. After 1 s held, the present now drains the presenter, takes the queue lock and makes the swapchain again (the
  same as F11). Backoff: 1 s, 2 s, then 4 s while the window stays hidden.
- Measured: none (a fix). Five player reports on an **RX 9070 XT, Windows, full screen, frame generation on (built-in
  or FSR 3)**: the picture froze after Alt+Tab, sometimes with no Alt+Tab. Linux RTX 3090 run of 12 FG/F11/minimize
  steps, all fine; new check `tests/test_present_held_image.py` (fails on their main).
- Ours: **we took #1458 + acquire-ahead on 2026-11-21 (dd29e83a)**, so we now have the hold without this way out. Our
  `kImageFreeNs` / `image_free` / `present_image_with_trial` (vk_replay.cpp ~5749-5967) match the context of their diff.
- Hardware: matters exactly on AMD's Windows driver (the owner's). NVIDIA waits inside the acquire, so it never holds.
- Port: ~30 lines in vk_replay.cpp (`kHeldRemakeNs`, `held_remake_due`, the remake branch in the hold) + their test
  (cuts the same functions as `test_present_image_free.py` / `test_present_acquire_ahead.py`). No randomizer file.
- Risk: low. It runs only after 1 s held, a case that is already broken. Check: full screen, FG on, Alt+Tab for 10 s and
  back, several times. Expect "the swapchain made again" in the log and the picture back within 1-5 s.

### 2. Handle tables end at the game's count: no more texture re-upload storms (b9bd74a2, #2258; RX_VK_HANDLE_TABLE_COUNT, on) - PORT FIRST
- What: a shader that picks a texture by its own index gets a 64-element descriptor array, and `stage_handles`
  computed all 64 from the game's light table. Only the first N records are this frame's lights (3 in Kokiri, ~20 in
  Castle Town by day, 40 by night). The records past N are leftovers whose slots the game has since given to other
  textures (a 3840x2160 scene picture, a 4096x4096 depth). Fetching them re-uploaded those textures from guest memory
  whenever a GPU write had marked them, frame after frame. Now the table's count (header word 0) is read, and only
  for a table that proves that layout (record stride, header at 16, word 2 = buffer capacity, one storage read per
  element on one line). Elements past the count get handle 0. Anything else is bound whole, as before.
- Measured (RTX 4070 SUPER, 4K DLAA, Windows): in the 5 s after each of three pause-menu closings, 6.4 / 10.7 GB
  uploaded -> 12 / 117 MB; frames over 16.7 ms 15 / 45 % -> 0.8 / 2.0 %; fps there 68.5 / 61.3 -> 76.1 / 75.2; Kokiri
  average 71.1 / 66.8 -> 75.9 / 75.4. On an RX 580 (RADV), "uploaded again" lines went 40 -> 2. It shows anywhere a
  released slot is reused, not only after the menu.
- Ours: same mechanism. `stage_handles` (vk_shader.cpp:871) computes every element, and vk_texture.cpp:4064 logs "the
  GPU wrote its storage ...: uploaded again". **First, at no cost:** in the installed build, run Kokiri, open and close
  the pause menu a few times, and count "uploaded again" lines in the log. That shows whether the storm happens here.
- Port: ~100 lines in vk_shader.cpp/.h + one call in vk_draw.cpp (pass `p.key`) + a switches.def row + their
  `tests/test_handle_table_count.py`. One dependency to adapt: `Shader::SimTurfStorageOrig(d)` (from #2093, which we
  lack) becomes our `d.cbuf_offset` for the buffer's size word (+8). `ub_read` exists here. No randomizer file.
- Risk: low to medium (a light or shadow missing where the layout check is fooled). Look at Castle Town at night,
  including the water near the lamps, and a lit Deku Stick and Din's Fire. Off switch `RX_VK_HANDLE_TABLE_COUNT=0`.

### 3. The GPU ASTC decoder rewritten: no scratch spills, block work hoisted out of the texel loop (658e5f2b, #2194) - PORT FIRST (shader only)
- What: `astc_decoder.comp` kept >512 B of dynamically indexed private arrays per thread, which spilled to scratch on
  AMD's compiler. They now live in packed LDS (`shared uint s_buf[16][65]`, no barriers). Partition, weight-grid and
  scaling work moves out of the per-texel loop, with a fast path when the grid equals the block. ISE range lookup is a
  binary search. `color_endpoint_data` is zero-initialised (a UB fix that the ASTC spec requires).
- Measured (Radeon 860M RDNA 3.5, Windows, 3 interleaved runs): 1365x1365x90 terrain arrays decode 2,309 -> 432 ms;
  save-load worst frame 2,423 -> 573 ms; load-to-play 10.8 -> 8.6 s; Kokiri -> Hyrule Field -> Market traversal 1%
  low 21.7 -> 32.3 fps (+48.5 %; Market 14.7 -> 28.6), frames >= 50 ms 32 -> 7, mean fps +2 %. Bit-exact against the
  reference over 1.7 M pixels and all 14 block sizes. Discrete-card numbers are still pending on their Test Farm.
- Ours: the 544229a2 decoder, unchanged (same blob e1831a0d). **The owner's RX 9070 XT has no hardware ASTC, so every
  ASTC upload here goes through this shader (then to BC3).** The gain on a 64-CU card will be smaller than on an
  860M, but area entries (Hyrule Field's terrain arrays) are exactly the owner's 1%-low problem.
- Port: replace `gpu/vk/host_shaders/astc_decoder.comp`, regenerate `astc_decoder_comp_spv.h` with glslang (do not
  hand-edit). It assumes #1553's two push constants `blocks_x, blocks_y` and the early return. **Our `AstcPush`
  (vk_texture.cpp:614) lacks them: add both, and set them at both push sites (lines ~1206 and ~1250) to the dispatch's
  block counts (g.tw, g.th).** Left at 0, every invocation returns at once and textures come out blank. Their
  `tests/astc_decoder_gpu.cpp` + `test_astc_decoder_gpu.py` come along (CPU mode runs without a GPU). No randomizer file.
- Measure: not only the fountain A/B. Also Hyrule Field entry (`vk: a frame's uploads took ... ASTC on the GPU`) and a
  save load. Visual check: terrain, distant hills, Market.
- Risk: low to medium (a wrong decode shows as corrupt textures; their bit-exact test covers it). Easy revert.

### 4. AMD Windows: multi-layer BC copies into non-4-aligned mip tails split per layer, so 8-layer ASTC-to-BC runs work (aafc8381, #2228; + the pitch half of ed602b94, #2015) - PORT, then A/B RX_VK_BC_LAYERS=8 at Hyrule Field entry
- What: the root cause of Hyrule Field's dark terrain grid lines on AMD's Windows driver. A `vkCmdCopyBufferToImage`
  region with layerCount > 1 into a BC mip whose size is not a multiple of 4 (e.g. 5x5) copies floor(w/4) x floor(h/4)
  blocks, so the right and bottom edge blocks stay black on every layer. Such copies are now split into one region per
  layer, in the same call (`bc_copy_per_layer`, on for AMD's Windows driver). #2015 also gives the buffer pitch
  explicitly (`bufferRowLength = blocks_x*4`, `bufferImageHeight = blocks_y*4`).
- Measured (RX 6700 XT, Windows, 26.10.1): with 8-layer runs back, the terrain arrays upload at Hyrule Field entry in
  2,106 -> 1,731 ms (-17.8 %), entry spike -400 ms. The split costs nothing (1,731 vs 1,746 ms). Beanstalk passed it on
  an RX 9060 XT (Windows) and an RX 9070/XT (RADV). Their source comment: "Hyrule Field's entry on an RX 9070 XT: 51 ms an
  array at one layer a run, 15 ms at 8" (with #1720's small scratch).
- Ours: our vk_texture.cpp:1217 comment says we run one layer at a time because of these exact grid lines ("RX 6600:
  none with one layer a run"). Our `bc_encode_into` (:931) still uses a single region with pitch 0,0.
  `RX_VK_BC_LAYERS=8` FAILED at the Market entrance and was NOISE at the fountain. That A/B measured steady fps, not
  entry uploads, and on the owner's driver its pictures may have had the grid lines.
- Port: ~20 lines in `bc_encode_into` (the split + explicit pitch), gated on the AMD proprietary driver id (we have no
  `device_policy.h`), and a switch row `RX_VK_BC_COPY_PER_LAYER`. Then re-test `RX_VK_BC_LAYERS=8`: Hyrule Field from
  the castle gate, the upload log line, and the distant hills by eye. Later, if it pays: #1720's small scratch and
  41be612f (#1987, a BC3 texture's mip levels re-encoded in groups, ~4/5 of the barriers gone).
- Risk: low (the split alone changes no result). The default stays 1 layer until the A/B says otherwise.

### 5. Global loads that cross the reserved-register boundary get their register map (0e32bc10, #2179) - PORT (small; batch with a translator change)
- What: the Ampere-to-Maxwell lowering checked only the first destination register of a vector LDG. A 128-bit load to
  R204 also writes R206-R207 (reserved), and those were not remapped. In frozen Zora's Domain (adult), the snow rocks by
  the pedestal flash pink and green. Fixes their issue #203.
- Measured: a fix. RTX 4070 SUPER, native mesh shaders and `RX_VK_MESH_SHADERS=compute`: before/after pictures.
- Ours: lower_to_maxwell.h:250 is the old line (`reg >= reserved_base`). The bug is in the translator, not tied to a
  GPU make.
- Port: 4 lines + the regmap_marker_test cases. It changes translated shaders, so the translator stamp changes and
  every shader is prepared again on first start. Bundle it with item 3 or another translator change. No randomizer file.
- Risk: very low.

### 10. Lift CPU work (9dd41326 #1905 A64_FPXKEEP; 4fc9eb65 #1871 A64_SUB_V) - LATER / skip
- FPXKEEP keeps NaN/inf operands and exact-zero FMAs off the software float routine: process CPU -0.8/-0.9 % in the
  Market at a 60 cap (RTX 3090), no fps claim. SUB_V writes the V flag as N xor (a<b), so signed compares become one host
  compare: 1.1 MB less code, **no fps gain** over 15 runs. Its idea comes from Rekarina, which we just added to our sources.
  Both need a full re-lift. They only pay together with VECFP (11-20 item 11), and only if the Market stays CPU-bound.
