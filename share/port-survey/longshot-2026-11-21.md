# Longshot v3.1.0 (2026-11-22, a2dc5385): is it worth testing here? (asked 2026-11-21)

The claim (release notes, no PR credited): Castle Town's Market on an RTX 3090, 2560x1440, no upscaling, uncapped:
v3.0.0 74 fps -> v3.1.0 101 fps (+37 %, median frame), 99th-percentile frame 29 -> 18 ms, 1 % low 18 -> 49 fps,
"most of it the game's own code: 9.2 -> 5.1 ms a frame". It is v3.0.0 against v3.1.0: everything between them.

## Verdict
Longshot v3.1.0's headline gain is mostly the renderer rewrite, which Longshot's own measurement on this card puts
below its old way in the owner's worst area. Not worth trying as a whole. Worth porting and A/B-ing here, one at a
time at the Market fountain: #1814 (OptimizeChangeBank hold), then VECFP (#1782, a re-lift), then #2085's barriers.
