# ProjectReLink survey, 2026-11-22 (16cba084..34efe843, "Version 0.7.9.3"; read only)

ProjectReLink (https://github.com/pixl27/ProjectReLink, GPL-3.0-or-later). 16cba084 ("Version 0.7.9.2") is in main's
history, so this is `git log 16cba084..origin/main`: 152 commits, 142 without merges (none patch-equal to one before),
111 files, +9,284 / -1,626 lines. About 100 of them are the 0792-gfx branch (dated 17-18/11, merged after 0.7.9.2), the
rest the 0.7.9.3 hotfix (19-22/11). Nothing built, run or installed. Our tree was checked by grep, and against the
owner's logs in `F:\Games\OOT Remake Rando\logs` (22/11).

Summary: most of the batch is already ours under the same switch names (the 18/11 shader-translation fixes, SSBO 16-byte
alignment, the cloth program without the optimizer, the CPU-side swapchain wait for hidden windows, UB word hash,
vertex-stream ranges, log batches, texture compaction throttle, shadow buckets, frame pacing, the detailed FPS counter
with its 1% low, upscaler rigid-object motion), or it serves hardware we don't have (no-mesh fallback, RX 6000, iGPUs,
Intel, NVIDIA DLSS-G/NGX). Five things are new and worth time here: fast nn::os mutexes in the game's own MutexType
(ReLink's only CPU-side speed item: +3.6 % in their Market), a camera-cut reset for the temporal upscalers (FSR 4's
ghosting after cuts), a fix for a freeze after the end credits when they play under 60 fps (the randomizer's players
finish the game), the striped-ground fix for the texture heap's compaction (we run that compaction too), and three cheap
hitch removals in long sessions (pool-map hash, retired pools set aside, the sync table's rehash).

## Candidates, best first

### 1. HOS_MUTEX_FAST: nn::os mutexes in a word of the game's MutexType (f7a5366, e3b1a92, e5379d9, 9d7d6a7; counters 8bd1d34/278254b): port first, behind an A/B
- What: LockMutex/UnlockMutex/TryLockMutex/IsMutexLockedByCurrentThread work on a 32-bit word at MutexType+8 (owner's
  small thread id, nesting depth, free/held/contended), one atomic op uncontended, WaitOnAddress when contended (vcore
  given up, 50 ms cap per wait). A mutex a condition variable waits with moves for good to the host object (bit 31), so
  the CV hand-off is unchanged. Thread ids come from a free list (9d7d6a7 fixed a reuse bug: copy it with the rest).
- Their numbers: the game locks ~3.5 M mutexes a second in the Market (~42,000 a frame); LockMutex was 14 % of the serial
  stretches, mostly the object lookup. Ryzen 5500 + RTX 3060 Ti: Market +3.6 % (3 alternated pairs), +12 % with draws
  skipped; Market plateau 87.0 fps (85.8-90.2 before). Their own note: "not validated for a release". On by default in
  0.7.9.3.
- Ours: not present. Our `obj()` has a 1024-entry per-thread cache (Hookshot's; theirs missed one time in eight), so the
  lookup share is likely smaller here, but the host object's lock line and the call stay. The Market here is game-CPU
  bound (2026-11-18 profile), which is where this helps. MutexType+8 is unused by our nn_os_sync.c (no HOS_MUTEX_IX).
- Hardware: CPU only, Windows only (off elsewhere). No AMD/VRAM concern.
- Port: ~200 lines into host/nn_os_sync.c (the HLE bodies of the mutex calls, two CV waits) and hos_thread.c (id free
  list); our helpers hos_vcore_away/back, hos_spin_ns exist. No randomizer or lift files.
- Risk: medium-high: every game mutex changes; a wrong nesting or wake path is a hang. Default off at first, then a
  Market + Kakariko A/B, a long tour (area changes, movies, the credits) and a randomizer seed run before turning it on.

### 2. Temporal upscalers reset on a camera cut found in the reprojection (596a723, the cut part): port first, tiny
- What: `dlss_math::camera_cut(reproject)` (15 lines): the picture's middle at three far depths, reprojected to the frame
  before, off the picture or behind the camera means a cut; the history is reset then. RX_VK_UPSCALER_CUT_RESET=0 off.
- Their numbers: nine synthetic cases right; a 20 s camera turn in the Market told no cut. Unmeasured for image quality.
- Ours: partly. vk_temporal.cpp ~1369 resets after two frames without inputs or a failed reprojection only; a cut in a
  cutscene, a door or a warp keeps FSR 4's history, which ghosts the scene before for some frames. Same chain heritage
  (hckrman101's), so it slots in where `reset` is set.
- Hardware: all engines (FSR 4 Native AA on this PC). No cost.
- Port: ~25 lines in gpu/vk/dlss_math.h and vk_temporal.cpp. Risk low (a false cut = one frame without history; check a
  fast camera turn says none). Look at a cutscene with it on and off.

### 3. No freeze after the end credits under 60 fps; 60 fps while a movie plays (7a2edb9): port first, small
- What: pictures behind the movie clock are skipped, so under 60 fps the credits' last movie (Dm_StaffCredit_0005.webm,
  7344 pictures) reached its end early: the event ended before "The End", its text stayed over the map and the next event
  (the prompt and button) waited forever. Now that movie's end is told only once the game has taken as many buffers as
  were skipped (HOS_VIDEO_END_HOLD; 0 off, 1 every movie). Above 60 fps the cap is held at 60 while a movie plays
  (HOS_VIDEO_UNLOCKED=1 off). Logs "fs: movie <name>" at each open.
- Their numbers: at 32 fps froze before, went to the title after; at 120 fps black screen before, fine after (RTX 3060 Ti,
  events run by hand). Player reports: issues 156, 157 (endings at 40-53 fps).
- Ours: same exposure. host/nn_video.c skips pictures to keep up with the sound (`skipped`, line ~574) and has no end
  hold; nn_vi.c lets the cap go above 60 during movies. A randomizer run ends with the credits; a dip under 60 there would
  freeze a finished seed.
- Port: ~90 lines in host/nn_fs.c, nn_vi.c, nn_video.c; our nn_video.c differs (decode stats) so a hand merge. No
  randomizer files, but test the ending (Debug tab or a save near Ganon) at a 30 fps cap and at 60.
- Risk: low; only the one movie's end changes by default.

### 4. Textures the GPU drew are written back before the texture heap's compaction moves them (40378d7): port, small, needs a seed check
- What: aglTextureDataMgr::Compaction moves a texture by a CPU memcpy then remakes it; a GPU-drawn texture (terrain
  normal-map tiles) had its texels only in our image, so the copy carried zeros and the ground turned to stripes after
  some minutes, until the area reloaded. Eight call sites (memcpy and a copier's virtual call in f_807dbf90, f_807db4a0,
  f_807dbd60) get `hos_tex_moving(from, size, to)`, which calls rx_nvn_vmap_changing (texture_flush_to_guest).
- Their numbers: Fire Temple, 4 min, compaction every frame: before stripes and all-zero tiles, after 0 of 550, 207
  textures written back in 186 ms total on the compaction thread.
- Ours: exposed. We run the same compaction (from Longshot, HOS_TEX_COMPACT_MS once a second in nx2/oot_patches.py) and
  already have texture_flush_to_guest / rx_nvn_vmap_changing. Same game binary (their compaction site main+0x777274 is
  our f_80797240). The owner has not reported stripes (bugs.md), and our once-a-second throttle makes moves rarer, but
  the bug class is the same.
- Port: ~30 lines host (oot_tex_compact.c-like function in our host file) plus 8 sites as text patches in
  nx2/oot_patches.py (a randomizer-listed file: a game rebuild and a seed run to check). Their lift uses `lifter.after`;
  ours patches the lifted C, so the sites need writing our way.
- Risk: low-medium (lift edit; a few ms on the compaction thread). Score: worth porting, after 1-3.

### 5. Hash for maps keyed by 64 KB-aligned pool starts (611960f): later, cheap
- What: an `AlignedKeyHash` (multiply, xor-fold) on pool_first_seen, the `seen` map in refresh_pool_ranges_locked,
  live_by_pool, direct_by_pool, direct_bytes_by_pool. With libc++ and a power-of-two bucket count, the identity hash put
  every aligned key in bucket 0.
- Their numbers: 17-23 ms stalls in a busy area's capture list (no fps figure).
- Ours: same maps, identity hash (vk_memory.cpp 1297, 1314, 4257-4259), same libc++ (llvm-mingw). libc++ picks prime
  bucket counts unless a reserve lands on a power of two (`seen.reserve(n)`), so the exposure may be partial here.
- Port: ~20 lines, vk_memory.cpp only. Risk very low. Check first with a log of the capture-list time.

### 6. Retired pools set aside once nothing needs them (bc19bc8): later
- What: retired pools with no import, mirror, shadow or pending write-back, the GPU past them, move to a second list and
  drop their page vectors; the walks at each submit and each guest free skip them. RX_VK_RETIRED_COMPACT=0 off.
- Their numbers: ~33 pools retired per area change (798 in 10 minutes); with 6000 padded pools the walks took 3.4 s of
  the render thread in ~3 minutes, 8 ms set aside.
- Ours: same exposure: `retired_pools` (vk_memory.cpp 4230) is walked in the free-range check (~4673), the give-backs
  (~5058, ~5130), trims_settle and mirror inheritance, and never shrinks. Long randomizer sessions are the case.
- Port: ~90 lines, vk_memory.cpp; our walks differ (GRACE give-back, inherit), so a hand port. Risk low-medium.

### 7. Sync-object table: a live byte per slot, so its rehash doesn't read every object (852e936): later, small
- What: the table doubles at half full; its rehash read each object (scattered) under the exclusive lock.
- Their numbers: 62.9 ms -> 6.85 ms at 524,288 slots (after ~11 areas); 0.9-4 ms -> 0.4-1.7 ms at start. A one-time
  hitch per doubling, every game thread waiting.
- Ours: same code (nn_os_sync.c insert_locked reads `table[i]->kind`). ~55 lines; HOS_SYNC_REHASH_OLD=1 off. Risk low.

### 8. Upscalers fed the composite before the game's SMAA, and their own sharpness under the HUD (05f8df9, 949d8dc, 596a723 sharpen part, 1d15836, 24a96f1): later, image quality
- What: the game blends SMAA into its composite before the upscale network, and the temporal upscalers were given that;
  they now take the composite (noted per draw by program key, `dlss_note_post_draw`), and a Sharpness of their own works on
  the 3D before the HUD (dlss_out.comp), default 40 %.
- Their numbers (RTX 3060 Ti, Market, mean luma step): FSR 3.1 5.8 -> 6.4, DLSS 5.8 -> 6.9 unsharpened; game's own 9.1-9.8.
  FSR 4 not tried (they have no RDNA 4).
- Ours: not present. vk_temporal.cpp takes "the colour as the network samples it", the same SMAA'd input. Our sharpening
  is on the finished picture.
- Port: medium (~200 lines plus a shader and its SPIR-V header regenerated; composite detection is new to us). Risk
  medium (a wrong composite = wrong colours). Worth a look with FSR 4 Native AA screenshots side by side.

### 9. A host-read barrier at the end of each replay command buffer (3db8e02): later, cheap correctness
- What: every recording ends with ALL_COMMANDS/MEMORY_WRITE -> HOST/HOST_READ, so the game reads what shaders wrote into
  guest memory (not only copies). RX_VK_NO_HOST_READ_BARRIER=1 off.
- Ours: partly: host barriers after capture copies and occlusion query copies only (vk_replay.cpp ~789, ~4484).
  Unmeasured; no symptom seen here. ~17 lines.

### 10. Fast sync's follow-ups (763cdfc, 85e4074): with the fast-sync port, not alone
- 763cdfc: captures wait again while a pool the CPU wrote is bound without a mirror (white grass on small-VRAM cards);
  85e4074: a stall watch thread so a lost device is reported with fast sync on. Take them together with f48ef92/e454c91
  if fast sync (still "waiting" from 11-19) is ported.

### 11. Low-RAM and diagnostics (later, not for this PC)

- 5ff1252 RX_VK_NEW_WAIT: renderer allocations wait up to 5 s at the commit limit. ~35 lines.
- 4aa719a: the cold-start pipeline prediction skips programs over 300k SPIR-V words (minutes and >10 GB of commit). Our
