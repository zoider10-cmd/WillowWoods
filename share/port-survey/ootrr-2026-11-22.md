# OOTRR (the other randomizer for this remake), 2026-11-22 (3ee77a6..a253c9a, 163 commits; read only)

Read with `git show`/`git diff` from a blobless clone; nothing of theirs built or run. Their releases 0.8.2 and the
0.8.3 nightlies fall in this range. About a third of the commits are deanrather's "difficulty mod" (more enemies,
storms, mega Like Likes, harder cuccos, owl rides) and web/nightly/release work; the rest is randomizer work by
Ontico, OruizGl and REALAZK. **The most important find is a bug in what we already took from them:** our Fire Temple
Goron removal (FIRE_GORONS, fixes 64, from their 97e343a) deletes the Room 17 Goron but leaves the **Boss Key chest**
locked; they found and fixed it in 7146d21. The second is a class of **missable checks** (the adult rolling Goron's
tunic, King Zora's thaw gift, the Membership Card) that close when a quest is moved on first; they keep them open
(92dbffd). Nothing in this range fixes bug 107 (young Link's tunics): theirs only lets a child *buy* a tunic.

## Candidates, best first

### 1. The Fire Temple's Boss Key chest stays locked with Skip cutscenes (fix for something we took) - port first
- Theirs: 7146d21 (`randomizer/core/src/fire_gorons.rs`), test 7542fab. Their note: the eight small key chests read
  their Goron's `Npc_Goron_NNN_Delete` straight into `Unlock`; **the Boss Key chest (Room 17,
  `Logic/FireTemple_Level_Room_17_Prefab_a4ce.logic.module.ainb`, `Obj_TBox_A_03_30dc`) reads an AND of
  `Npc_Goron_010_Delete` and "the player is outside Area_ccd2"**. Only that Goron's talk sets the flag, so with the
  Goron deleted the chest stays locked and gives no prompt. They force its `Unlock` to constant true.
- Ours: `tools/rando/rando_seed.py` FIRE_GORONS lists room 17's Goron (`17_Prefab_68e8`), but FIRE_CHESTS has only the
  eight `_A_01` chests (no `17_Prefab_a4ce`). With Skip cutscenes on, a new seed's Fire Temple Boss Key chest can
  never be opened, so the Fire Temple can't be finished. Not in share/bugs.md yet. Likely no one has reached it in play
  since fixes 64.
- Port: add `"17_Prefab_a4ce"` to FIRE_CHESTS. `ainb_fix_inputs(data, "Unlock", "ActorLogicTreasureBox", 1)` should
  fit (one linked Unlock input, from the AND); check the file has exactly one ActorLogicTreasureBox, otherwise the call
  stops with a clear error. A FIXES_VERSION bump, seed tool only, no game rebuild. Their test also checks that every
  Fire Temple chest whose module reads a Goron delete flag is unlocked: a check like that in `tests/` would have
  caught this. Risk: very low. **Score: first, today.** Add it to share/bugs.md as a new bug.

### 2. Missable story gifts: the adult rolling Goron, King Zora thawed, the Membership Card - first
- Theirs: 92dbffd (`open_world/quest_order.rs`, 502 lines; `open_world/fortress.rs`, 505 lines). Facts from their
  notes (read from the game files, worth checking in ours):
  - **Link the Goron (Goron Tunic)** gives his gift only while `SageOfFire` is at `TalkDaruniaJr`. Entering the Crater
    (MeetSheik step: Bolero, or the trail), the temple or the quest's end moves it on, and he then talks through
    `Npc_Goron_DaruniaJr_Adult` (`Talk`, `Npc_Goron_DaruniaJr_Step3_on`, `SageOfFire_IsCompleted_Exp_Talk`) with no
    gift. Their fix: a branch in front of those entries; while `SageOfFire_Step02_DoorOpen` is false it calls
    `SageOfFire:Step02_Npc_Goron_DaruniaJr_Adult_Talk` (the gift). Also: entering Goron City from the Crater plays no
    intro, so `SageOfFire_GenerateDaruniaJr` is never set and he is missing; they set it on the
    `Dm_SY_BancChangeGo` `DeathMountainCrater_ToGoronCity` exit for an adult.
  - **King Zora thawed (Zora Tunic)**: after the Water Temple he talks through `Npc_Zora_KingZora_adult` `Talk` with no
    gift; they branch to `SageOfWater:Safety_Entry` while `SageOfWater_KingZora_adult_Talk_Event198` is false.
  - **Membership Card**: the card scene `Dm_Gerudo_0025` moves SageOfSpirit Step01 to Step02. Reaching the Colossus or
    the Spirit Temple first moves the quest further, which also clears the fortress, so the card was missed.
  - Asking a quest for an earlier step **moves it back** (Fire or Spirit progress undone). They guard seven step
    requests (`ORDER`: SageOfFire StopDaruniaJr/TalkDaruniaJr/MoveStatue; SageOfSpirit Step01, Step02 and Step04 in
    `Npc_Hylia_012_Adult`, `Npc_Gerudo_006`, `SageOfSpirit`, `Dm_SY_BancChangeArrive`) to run only while the quest is
    not already past that step.
- Ours: `goron_tunic` (`SageOfFire`, GROWN|{BB}) and `zora_tunic` (`SageOfWater`, EVERYTHING) are shuffled gifts
  (bug 65's fix, placement 12). The logic can't stop a player going to the Crater first (Bolero, or a Goron Tunic found
  elsewhere) or doing the Water Temple before the thaw (a Zora Tunic found elsewhere). Either one loses a check, which
  may hold a progression item. The card isn't shuffled in ours, so only the step guards matter there (the Requiem can
  take Link to the Colossus first, bug 26's area).
- Port: flow edits of the kind we already make with `tools/rando/evfl` (as for Epona: new events in front of entries).
  Medium size (Goron and Zora first, about 100-150 lines in rando_seed.py). Touches the randomizer's flows only, no
  host or lift change. Risk: medium. A wrong branch could repeat a gift, so read the flows in our dump first and test
  with a save past each step. **Score: first** (the Goron one is the most likely to bite).

### 3. Forest Temple Bow room: the door opens when the Stalfos die - later

  **Score: later**, unless the owner gets stuck there.
