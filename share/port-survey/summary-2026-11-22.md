# Survey of all ports, 2026-11-22: the combined list

Seven sources had moved: HeyListen, ProjectReLink, Longshot, Reborn engine, Triforce, Rekarina (new, first survey) and
OOTRR (the other randomizer). Fritchii and upstream Mako had not; LICHII still has no link. Each port's full report is
beside this file (`<port>-2026-11-22.md`). Read only: nothing of theirs was built or run.

Already done from this survey: **bug 108**, the Fire Temple's Boss Key chest locked with Skip cutscenes (found by
OOTRR 7146d21; fixed in a191fddc, installed).

## Ranked: what to port, for this PC (RX 9070 XT, Windows 11, FSR 4)

Every speed item is A/B'd on this PC before it's kept (the Market or Kakariko, the port-survey skill's gate): the
other ports' numbers are mostly from NVIDIA cards.

### A. Fixes for problems that can hit the owner (first)
1. **Freeze after Alt+Tab with frame generation** (Longshot 577bba12): a side effect of the held-image change we took on
   2026-11-21, on AMD Windows. ~30 lines, renderer.
2. **Credits freeze under 60 fps** (ReLink 7a2edb9): the last credits movie ends early and the game freezes after "The
   End" when the credits run below 60 fps. Every randomizer run ends there. ~90 lines, host.
3. **Lock-free queue crash** (Rekarina "ringpost"): the game's queues abort when a writer is late; crashed a player
   after the first boss. Small lift change (nx2/oot_patches.py: needs the randomizer check after).
4. **Gifts lost by doing things out of order** (OOTRR 92dbffd): the adult rolling Goron's Goron Tunic if the Crater is
   entered first; King Zora's thaw gift if the Water Temple is done first. Seed tool (flow edits).
5. **Upscaler history kept across camera cuts** (ReLink 596a723): FSR 4 ghosts for a few frames after cutscene cuts,
   doors and warps. ~15 lines.

### B. Stutter at area changes (measured on AMD by Reborn; four ports share the first)
6. **Capture spare buffers refilled, made off the lock** (Triforce cap_spare.h + RX_VK_CAP_FREE_ASYNC; Reborn
   adcb6f7, 5d00dad): ~1 s over three runs of slow buffer makes at scene loads on an RX 7900 XTX. ~150 lines.
7. **Idle mirrors freed a few at a time** (Reborn adcb6f7): the game waited 90 and 477 ms when a load's mirrors were
   freed at once. ~25 lines.
8. **Texture re-upload storms after the pause menu** (Longshot b9bd74a2): 6-11 GB re-uploaded; 61-68 -> 75 fps there
   (RTX 4070 S). Check our log first (count "uploaded again" after closing the menu in Kokiri). ~100 lines.
9. **GPU ASTC decoder 5x faster** (Longshot 658e5f2b): this card always decodes ASTC on the GPU. Area-entry stalls 4x
   shorter, 1 % low +48 % on a Radeon 860M. Makes every player prepare shaders again: batch with Longshot 0e32bc10
   (the frozen Zora's Domain rock flashing fix, 4 lines).
10. **ASTC-to-BC grid lines' real cause** (Longshot aafc8381 + the pitch half of ed602b94): makes 8-layer runs safe
    (-375 ms at Hyrule Field entry on an RX 6700 XT). ~20 lines, then A/B RX_VK_BC_LAYERS=8.

### C. Frame rate (A/B each)
11. **Fast game mutexes** (ReLink HOS_MUTEX_FAST): +3.6 % Market (RTX 3060 Ti). Risk of hangs; their own note says not
    release-ready. Behind a switch, off until A/B'd.
12. **Index buffer bound once** (Triforce RX_VK_INDEX_REBASE): ~2,900 fewer driver calls a frame in the Market.
    AMD's driver is slow per call, so it may help here. ~40 lines.
13. **No-build A/B: RX_VK_READBACK_LATE=3980,40,2000** (Longshot): -8 to -11 % frame time on a 9070 XT (Linux). Then
    HeyListen's readback ring only if it shows a gain.
14. Small: HeyListen RX_VK_DRAW_HPTR; ReLink's three long-session hitch removals (611960f, bc19bc8, 852e936).

### D. To check on this PC, no port yet
- **AMD's pipeline cache limit** (Rekarina's notes: ~850 MiB kept, nothing new past it; we hand it ~1.2 GB). The
  owner's log shows whether pipelines are rebuilt each start.
- **Render-target heap by the Market's gate** (froze Rekarina): walk that room on the owner's settings.

### E. Randomizer ideas
- **Bug 107 (young Link's tunics):** Reborn's ocarina picker swaps the slot's actor name in `EquipmentUser::update`
  (0x82e16c80, our build too) just before the game makes the actor: a worked example for the tunics. OOTRR lets a
  child *buy* tunics in the Goron and Zora shops (DisplayShop Event169), which goes with it.
- **OOTRR's newer dungeon logic**: refresh our copy (tools/rando/ootrr) and compare; e.g. the Deku Tree basement back
  room's Skulltula also needs fire, the Bow or explosives for the web.
- **Forest Temple Bow room opens when the Stalfos die** (OOTRR), instead of our warp-out.
- Settings we lack (OOTRR): faster Ocarina memory game, any fish wins, one Dampe race for both prizes, skip the tower
  escape, the adult trade on its items alone, gravedigging and the sun shot as checks.

### F. Features others have
- Rekarina: dash without the roll first; automatic save import from emulators and other ports; skippable unskippable
  cutscenes; dialogue without its pauses.
- ReLink: an upscaler Sharpness row and "Upscaler: None". Reborn: unlimited amiibo scans. Triforce: achievements, the
  game's audio effects (reverb, delay: already on our list).
- Longshot: input record and replay, reworked Sprint, grass distance.

## Skipped (see each report)
