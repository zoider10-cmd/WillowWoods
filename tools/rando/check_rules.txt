# Check rules: what a check asks for, where the remake differs from the rules the randomizer came with.
#
# This file comes with the tools. Your own lines go in  my_check_rules.txt  in the game folder's "rando" folder
# (made there the first time a seed is written): a build never writes over that one. They count for NEW seeds; a
# seed already made keeps the rules it was made with.
#
# A line:    <area> | <check, as the Tracker names it> | add: <words>      the check asks for these too
#            <area> | <check, as the Tracker names it> | only: <words>     the check asks for these and nothing else
#
# Words: items by the Tracker's names (Hookshot, Longshot, Bomb Bag, Boomerang, Fairy Slingshot, Fairy Bow,
#   Megaton Hammer, Iron Boots, Hover Boots, Goron's Bracelet, Silver Gauntlets, Golden Gauntlets, Silver Scale,
#   Golden Scale, Mirror Shield, Lens of Truth, Kokiri Sword, Master Sword, Din's Fire, Fire Arrows, Magic, Bottle,
#   Goron Tunic, Zora Tunic, Zelda's Letter, Ocarina, and the songs: Zelda's Lullaby, Epona's Song, Saria's Song,
#   Sun's Song, Song of Time, Song of Storms, Bolero of Fire, Minuet of Forest, Serenade of Water, Requiem of Spirit,
#   Nocturne of Shadow, Prelude of Light).
#   A comma or "and" between things all needed; "or" between different ways.
#   adult     only an adult can get it (the way to an adult is asked for, and the Tracker lists it as an adult's)
#   child     the Tracker lists it as a child's
#   nothing   it asks for nothing
#
# A chest or Gold Skulltula without a name of its own is told by its place, as its Tracker row says it:
#     Hyrule Field | Gold Skulltula at -40 158 201 | only: Kokiri Sword or Fairy Slingshot or Boomerang or adult
#
# Examples:
#     Goron City | Chest (red rupee) | add: Megaton Hammer
#     Dodongo's Cavern | Chest (red rupee), room 5 | only: Bomb Bag or Goron's Bracelet
#
# Text after a "#" is a note. A line the tool cannot read is left out, and making a seed says which.
