## What this means for later picks

After be663a08, Longshot's host/oot_* files live in game/. After 3072dda4 its lift patches are Rust (the `sites` and
`edits` crates), not nx2/oot_patches.py. Later picks of the game's C need a path map (game/X.c -> host/X.c). Later
lift fixes must be re-expressed as our Python patches: harder, and more often "idea only". The `base` note in
sources.json may want a line saying so.
